# FEA readiness research: literature, standards, and open implementations

Research freeze: 2026-10-30 UTC. This is a read-only source review for a standardized FEA preprocessor, theory pre-calculation, and result anomaly diagnosis. `sources.json` is the machine-readable provenance record: every counted source has a public URL, retrieval hash, readable scope, applicability boundary, and license/rights note. The downloaded cache is local evidence only and is not part of the public package.

I inspected public text, PDFs, HTML pages, and pinned source files without importing or executing external code, installing packages, changing client configuration, or starting a solver. “Verified” below means that the stated content was found in the retrieved artifact. It does not mean that a third-party solver was run or that its claims were independently reproduced.

## What the standards imply for the module

NASA-STD-7009B (NASA-STD-7009B, 2024) requires the model record to retain units and vector frames, assumptions and abstractions, limits of operation, verification and validation domains, numerical error estimates, and aspects that remain unverified. It distinguishes implementation/code verification, per-use solution verification, conceptual validation, and empirical validation. ASME V&V 10 (2019, reaffirmed 2025) provides the same CSM VVUQ vocabulary and framework; only its public product description and the public V&V10.1 description were read because the standard text is paid. NAFEMS states that vendor benchmark verification should be independently checked by users. Its public R0135 description emphasizes observed asymptotic order from multiple meshes and an exact solution, without requiring source access.

The module should therefore maintain four separate states: `input_verified`, `solution_verified`, `physics_validated`, and `uncertainty_characterized`. A successful parse, positive Jacobian, or converged Newton iteration can only update the first two. A physical reference such as Hertz, a gear formula, or a test measurement belongs to validation and must carry its assumptions and domain.

NASA's public grid-convergence tutorial gives a concrete numerical rule: three or more resolutions are needed to estimate observed order and to test the asymptotic range; the last-two-grid difference alone ignores refinement ratio and can be misleadingly small. Richardson extrapolation and GCI require a smooth asymptotic expansion, a meaningful normalization, and care near discontinuities. The module should report `h`, refinement ratio, observed order, asymptotic-range indicator, extrapolated value, and the reference quantity; it should reject “converged” when these fields are unavailable.

## Gear and contact evidence

NASA NTRS 19920014293, *Contact stresses in meshing spur gear teeth: Use of an incremental finite element procedure* (Hsieh, Huston, Oswald, 1992; public report, DOI not assigned), models 2D plane strain, small displacement, linearly elastic gear teeth and Coulomb friction cases. Its contact extent is unknown at the start and is established incrementally. The report compares a spur-gear FE result with Hertz and shows that pressure can be about 10% above Hertz in the examined case. This supports tracking active contact boundaries and load increments; it does not provide a universal gear tolerance.

NASA NTRS 19930019222, *Effect of extended tooth contact on the modeling of spur gear transmissions* (Lin, Wang, Oswald, Coy, 1993), explains that tooth flexibility can make an incoming pair engage earlier and an outgoing pair disengage later. Ignoring this extended contact underestimates contact zone and mean mesh stiffness and overstates individual tooth load, especially for heavily loaded low-contact-ratio gears. An extra active tooth or tip pressure therefore requires an active-contact and deformation check before it is called penetration.

NASA NTRS 20150016602 / DETC2015-46274, *Gear Tooth Root Stresses of a Very Heavily Loaded Gear Pair* (Krantz, Handschuh, 2015), reports that theoretical HPSTC single-tooth loading can be inadequate when elastic deformation changes tooth-to-tooth load sharing. Its specific internal/external actuator case gives HPSTC-to-contact-analysis maximum-root-stress ratios of 1.40 for the ring and 1.28 for the pinion. The ratios are case results, not correction factors for another gear.

NASA NTRS 19820002528, *A multi-purpose method for analysis of spur gear tooth loading*, resolves loaded contact positions and load sharing iteratively. The compliance includes bending, normal and shear deformation, hub rocking, rim/hub torsion, and local Hertz deformation. A rigid line-of-action model is consequently a narrower reference model, not a general loaded-gear truth.

Code_Aster's public SSNV104 sphere-contact verification case is a particularly useful template. It states the Hertz assumptions (`a << R`, frictionless contact), defines geometry/material/loading, and separates `ANALYTIQUE`, `NON_REGRESSION`, and `AUTRE_ASTER` references. Model A deliberately supplies a reference solution, while other models test contact formulations and geometric updating. The synthesis says smoothing, master/slave normal averaging, and geometric contact convergence affect pressure and symmetry. Its source test files use separate analytic tolerances and regression values; that separation should be retained in M37.

## Rule mapping for the planned modules

| Module area | Rule or output | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input identity | Freeze case, generator, deck/meta, solver version/hash, units, frames, assumptions, and intended use. Re-read the exported deck and hash it before solve. | NASA-STD-7009B M&S 10-13, 45-46. This proves pedigree and declared semantics, not physical validity. |
| Theoretical pre-calculation | Emit Hertz/gear reference values with assumptions: geometry regime, friction, `a/R`, linear elasticity, small displacement, plane strain/3D, active tooth-pair hypothesis. Mark reference as `ANALYTIC_REFERENCE`, never as a solved result. | Code_Aster SSNV104; ContactMechanics; NASA 19920014293. Outside assumptions, classify as `REFERENCE_OUT_OF_DOMAIN`. |
| Gear loaded contact | Track theoretical contact arc and deformed active contact separately. Report entry/exit shifts, active tooth count, contact-zone extent, load share, and local pressure. | NASA 19930019222 and 19820002528. Extra contact can be elastic extended contact; it is not automatically initial overlap. |
| Mesh quality | Check TRI6/QUAD8 full mapped Jacobian candidate extrema, shared midside identity, non-manifold edges, aspect/conditioning and contact-region resolution. Store method and minimum location. | NAFEMS R0135; current independent finite checks. Positive Jacobian is necessary, not pressure convergence or stiffness sufficiency. |
| Mesh convergence | At least three controlled resolutions where possible; store `h`, ratio, QOI, observed order, asymptotic test, Richardson/GCI and normalized error. Require the same physical definitions and contact/BC semantics at each mesh. | NAFEMS R0135 and NASA spatial tutorial. Last-two-grid closeness alone is not a convergence gate; changing contact topology invalidates a simple order fit. |
| Actual contact | Store master/slave, method, normal convention, gap/overlap, ADJUST/penalty, active sets by increment, and contact quadrature/pressure. Distinguish geometric screening, initial gap, and deformed contact. | Code_Aster, Tribol, SfePy, dolfinx-contact, NASA gear studies. A local nearest point or polygon screen cannot certify global curved nonpenetration. |
| Boundary and constraints | Expand named sets/equations; check intended rigid modes, dependent DOF conflicts, prescribed closure/rotation, and step-level replacement. Reject unknown keyword attributes. | Code_Aster command tests and existing M37 audit. A nonempty BC list or solver convergence is not sufficient stability evidence. |
| Applied loads | Compute signed resultant `F` and moment `M = sum((x-x0) cross f)` from exported cards, about a declared origin and current/reference configuration. Track load decomposition by step and patch. | pyNastran tests, Tribol force diagnostics, NASA contact procedure. Reject partial/unsupported load types and compare vectors/moments, not magnitudes only. |
| Reaction/contact balance | Compare same-body RF and contact resultant with equal sign convention; shift moments to the same origin; record force and moment residuals independently. | CalculiX definition from prior source review plus Tribol tests. A scalar normal projection is not automatically pressure integral or ideal `T/rb`. |
| Energy/work | For a conservative contact path, integrate `W = integral F dot du` over increments and report elastic, contact penalty, frictional/dissipated and external work separately. Do not use a fixed `F delta/2` identity for nonlinear Hertz contact; Hertz gives `2F delta/5`. | ContactMechanics Hertz reference, SfePy/dolfinx nonlinear examples, NASA-STD numerical-error record. Missing energy components are `NOT_EVALUATED`, not zero. |
| Newton/solver result | Record final increment/time, residual norm, increment norm, line-search/active-set changes, and whether convergence was residual or incremental. Reject stale `.dat/.sta` and outputs without target step. | dolfinx Newton implementation and prior CalculiX review. Exit code or a nonempty output file is not a physical completion gate. |
| Anomaly classification | Classify separately: `INPUT_IDENTITY`, `UNSUPPORTED_CARD`, `UNITS_FRAME`, `MATERIAL_SECTION`, `BC_RIGID_MODE`, `MESH_JACOBIAN`, `INITIAL_GAP`, `ACTIVE_CONTACT_TOPOLOGY`, `LOAD_FORCE`, `LOAD_MOMENT`, `RF_CF_BALANCE`, `ENERGY_PATH`, `SOLVER_CONVERGENCE`, `MESH_CONVERGENCE`, `REFERENCE_OUT_OF_DOMAIN`, `PHYSICAL_VALIDATION_OPEN`. | The standards require recording limits and unverified aspects; gear/contact sources explain why apparent anomalies can be load sharing or active-contact effects. Classification is diagnostic evidence, not root-cause proof. |

## Limits and reproducibility

The public package should contain only this report, `sources.json`, and `repositories.json`. `sources.json` records 10 literature/official sources and 6 new implementations, 52 retrieved artifacts, fixed commit/DOI/URL information where available, readable ranges, hashes, and rights notes. NASA NTRS sources are public records; one gear-root PDF states U.S. Government public release, while the extended-contact PDF retains an AIAA copyright notice, so the original PDFs are not redistributed here. ASME and NAFEMS full standards/manuals remain copyright or member-only and were not downloaded. Code_Aster source is GPL; third-party source caches remain local and are not part of the implementation package.

The reviewed sources establish a disciplined evidence structure, not a universal acceptance threshold. Any future change to element order, contact method, penalty/ADJUST, load patch, material, solver, reference formula, or QOI must re-run input verification, solution verification, and the applicable validation/convergence study separately.
