16.1 Conventions

- Specifications with .m mean: Mean value within tolerance.

- The logarithmically interpolated value must be entered for factors ZL, ZV, ZR, ZW, ZX, YδrelT, YRrelT and YX. This value is calculated from the 
infinite life strength and static strength values, based on the number of load cycles.
